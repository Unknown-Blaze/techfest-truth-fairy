🧚 Truth Fairy - User Guide 🧚

Everything you need to know about using Truth Fairy to combat misinformation.

Getting Started

  1. Install the Extension:

    Download Truth Fairy from the Chrome Web Store, or see what you would like with that. Make sure the code is good.

  2. Pin the Icon (Optional):

    Pin the extension icon to your Chrome toolbar for quick access. To do this, click the extensions puzzle piece icon, find Truth Fairy, and click the pin icon next to it.

  3. Start Browsing: Truth Fairy works automatically in the background as you browse the web.

Analyzing Text for Misinformation

  1. Select the text: Use your mouse to select the section of text you want to check for credibility.
  2. Wait to flag: After selecting the text, click the Flag!
  3. Right-Click for Options: Right-click on the highlighted text to reveal the context menu.
  4. View Analysis: In the context menu, you will see the details.
    • Credibility Score: A percentage indicating how likely the text is to be accurate.
    • Justification: A simple and short data that you have added, depending on what to create, you may have something This is going to be where is your information
    • Discuss With Others: Connect with other users to share your views on why it's fake and what made you think so.

Analyzing Images for Manipulation (Deepfakes)

  1. Hover:Hover your mouse over the image you wish to scan. The flag will be in the top right of the image.
  2. Press the button: To scan an image
  3. Observe The Border: Based on the AI and past experience, the border will show you if what we believe is true or not
  4. Discuss With Others: Connect with other users to share your views on why it's fake and what made you think so.

How to Join Community Discussions

  1. Flag content first!!: First you can right click to view the options!
  2. Then view information with others. In addition to helping you, the purpose is all about discussion!
